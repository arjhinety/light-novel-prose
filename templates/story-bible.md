# Template: Story Bible

> **Purpose:** The single source of truth for a light-novel project, holding the premise, POV, cast, address forms, leitmotifs, running jokes, and open threads, so every chapter stays consistent.
> **Load when:** Starting a new story (fill it in before Chapter 1), resuming a story (rebuild it from the existing text first), or any time you are unsure what a character calls someone.
> **Related:** [Character Sheet](character-sheet.md), [Chapter Plan](chapter-plan.md), [Character Design](../cast/character-design.md), [Archetypes](../cast/archetypes.md), [Honorifics & Address](../dialogue/honorifics-and-address.md), [Series Planning](../structure/series-planning.md), [Arc Design](../structure/arc-design.md), [School Life](../setting/school-life.md), [Genre Index](../genres/genre-index.md)

---

## 1. How to use this template

1. **Copy the blank template (§2)** into the working notes for the project. Fill in every field. Write "TBD" rather than leaving a field empty, so gaps stay visible.
2. **Keep it short per field.** A bible that takes an hour to read won't get read. One line per field unless a field says otherwise. The details of any single character belong in their [Character Sheet](character-sheet.md).
3. **Update after every chapter.** At minimum, update the *Address Ledger* (§2.5), *Running Jokes & Callbacks* (§2.7), and *Open Questions* (§2.9). These are the three fields that drift, and drift is what breaks immersion.
4. **When continuing someone else's text, extract before you invent.** Read the existing chapters and fill the bible from what is actually on the page: names, suffixes in use, tics, and the leitmotif. Only then add new material. Never overwrite an established fact because you like your idea better.
5. **If the story uses an existing series (fan fiction, parody, crossover),** fill the *Canon Sheet* (§2.12) for every canon character **before** anything else, following [Fan Fiction & Canon](../adaptation/fanfic-and-canon.md). The library's default cast is example material only. Never copy its traits (hair, tics, roles) onto canon characters.
6. **If the user says "just write it",** fill the bible yourself with bold, specific choices, and list the 3-5 most important ones in a short note after the chapter (see the delivery rules in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)).

Why each section exists:

| Section | What goes wrong without it |
|---|---|
| Premise & core question | The POV's monologue wanders; chapters stop feeling like one book. |
| POV profile | The narrator's voice drifts toward generic, neutral, "helpful" narration. |
| Cast table | Heroines blur together; two characters end up with the same tic. |
| Address ledger | "Kuze-kun" becomes "Kuze-san" in chapter 9, and readers notice. |
| Leitmotif | Emotional peaks have no image to escalate, so they fall flat. |
| Running jokes | Callbacks are forgotten, and the payoff chapters feel random. |
| Setting & calendar | Events happen in the wrong season; the school feels generic. |
| Open questions | Mysteries get dropped or answered twice. |
| Tone dials | Comedy/drama balance swings chapter to chapter. |

## 3. Filled example: the default cast

This is the bible for the story in [Sample Chapter 1](../examples/sample-chapter-01.md), [Sample Chapter 2](../examples/sample-chapter-02.md), and the [Climax Chapter](../examples/sample-chapter-climax.md).

### 3.1 Premise

- **Working title:** *Do You Like the Go-Home Club?*
- **Genre(s):** School drama wearing a harem rom-com costume. Comedy up front, bullying arc underneath.
- **Logline:** A loner who keeps a mental ledger of every social debt tries to work out why his oblivious classmate is adored by four girls, until a tsundere drafts him as her lunch taste-tester.
- **Core question:** "What exactly is so special about *him*?"
- **The lie:** Everything can be kept even. Rational means safe.
- **The truth:** Some things aren't debts. Caring about someone doesn't need a better reason.
- **Target:** ~100 chapters of 3,000-6,000 words. Arc 1 = April-June (Ch. 1-40).
- **Rating:** Teen drama. Bullying, embarrassment, jealousy. No sexual content.

### 3.2 POV profile

- **Name:** Minato Kuze. The narration uses "Kuze".
- **Year/class/club:** 2nd year, Class 2-3, go-home club.
- **Defining flaw:** Treats his feelings as accounting problems, and calls cowardice "rationality".
- **Engine:** The ledger. He counts cans, turns, and favors, and he rereads social media posts like contracts.
- **Speech register:** Terse. Two to six words per line. Deadpan. Corrects small factual errors.
- **Stress tells:** Grips things too hard, rereads the index of a textbook, walks one station early.
- **Comforts:** Black coffee from the second-floor vending machine. Arriving at 8:14. The spot behind the pool building.
- **Confidant:** Daichi Enomoto.
- **Would never say aloud:** "I want someone to fight over me too."

### 3.6 Leitmotif

- **Primary:** "the sediment", silt settling at the bottom of Kuze's chest, one grain at a time.
- **Escalation:** Ch. 1, it settles. Ch. 2, it stirs and settles into a new shape. Mid-arc, it thickens. Ch. 31 opening, "no more room for it to fall." Outburst, "they weren't settling anymore. They were pouring."
- **Secondary:** the ledger (entries that close themselves), the navy-blue lunch cloth, black coffee, socks on a dojo floor.

### 3.8 Setting

- Seiryou Private High School, a mid-sized city with a river and a train line along the hills.
- Class 2-3. Kuze sits in the back row, four rows behind Amamiya.
- Key places: the second-floor vending machine, the roof (broken lock), the Literature and Broadcast room in the old building, behind the pool building, and the riverbank cherry trees.
- Parody brands: **Chirp** (social app).
- Calendar: Arc 1 runs April to late May. Arc 2 is built on cultural-festival prep (June to September).

### 3.11 Style notes

- The bell is always `Ding-dong-dang-dong.` on its own line.
- Titles: "Do You Like X? N".
- Sections are numbered with bare digits. Chapters open with an unnumbered section.
- Banned in this book: "a testament to", "little did he know", "a mix of emotions", "he couldn't help but" (max once per chapter).
